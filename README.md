# Inventory Control & Replenishment Analysis | Excel Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing inventory performance, stock availability, replenishment requirements, and supplier efficiency using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to improve inventory visibility, identify stockout risks, evaluate inventory movement, and support data-driven purchasing and replenishment decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor inventory levels and stock availability.
- Identify low-stock and out-of-stock products.
- Calculate reorder quantities to support replenishment planning.
- Evaluate inventory turnover and identify slow-moving products.
- Analyze inventory distribution across product categories.
- Assess supplier lead times and potential procurement risks.
- Support inventory planning using demand forecasts.

## Dataset Description

The project uses a simulated retail inventory dataset containing:

- **60 products** across six product categories.
- **10 suppliers** with varying lead times.
- **1,200 stock movement records** covering purchases, sales, returns, and damages.
- Product-level information including opening stock, reorder levels, target stock, unit costs, and monthly demand forecasts.

The dataset was designed to represent practical inventory management and replenishment scenarios.

## Key Findings

### 1. Inventory Availability

The analysis identified 2 out-of-stock products and 8 low-stock products.

Together, these represent **16.7% of the 60-product inventory catalog**, highlighting products requiring replenishment attention.

### 2. Inventory Valuation

Total current inventory was valued at approximately **KES 33.7 million**, representing 7,920 units across six product categories.

### 3. Inventory Turnover

The average inventory turnover ratio was **1.32**, providing a baseline for assessing inventory movement and identifying relatively slow-moving products.

The dashboard highlights the ten products with the lowest inventory turnover ratios for further review.

### 4. Replenishment Requirements

Ten products were identified as requiring replenishment based on their current stock levels and reorder thresholds.

Reorder quantities were calculated to help restore inventory toward target stock levels.

### 5. Product Category Distribution

Inventory levels vary across the six product categories: Accessories, Electronics, Hardware, Home & Kitchen, Office Supplies, and Personal Care.

Category-level analysis provides visibility into stock distribution and supports inventory allocation decisions.

### 6. Supplier Lead Time Analysis

Supplier lead times vary across the supplier network, with the longest lead time shown on the dashboard reaching approximately **18 days**.

These differences highlight the importance of considering delivery timelines when planning replenishment.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritize critical replenishment:** Address the two out-of-stock products first, followed by the eight low-stock products, to reduce potential sales disruptions.

2. **Improve reorder planning:** Use reorder thresholds, target stock levels, and monthly demand forecasts to support purchasing decisions.

3. **Monitor slow-moving inventory:** Review products with low inventory turnover to identify potential overstocking and opportunities for better inventory allocation.

4. **Strengthen supplier coordination:** Consider supplier lead times when scheduling purchase orders, particularly for products sourced from suppliers with longer delivery periods.

5. **Optimize inventory distribution:** Compare stock levels across product categories against expected demand to identify potential imbalances.

6. **Monitor stock coverage:** Use monthly demand forecasts to estimate how long current stock will last and identify products at risk of running out before replenishment.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to analyze inventory performance and support practical procurement and replenishment decisions.

Through inventory calculations, Pivot Tables, supplier analysis, and interactive dashboard development, the project transforms operational data into actionable insights that can support inventory control, stock optimization, and purchasing efficiency.
